# AI & Prompt Engineering Projects

## Overview

This repository collection contains four projects focused on **Artificial Intelligence, Research, and Prompt Engineering**. The projects demonstrate the use of AI for research, advanced prompt design, business problem-solving, and practical prompt development.

## Projects Included

1. **AI Research & Citation**
   Explores AI-assisted research, information verification, reliable sources, fact-checking, and proper citation.

2. **Advanced Prompt Engineering**
   Demonstrates advanced techniques for creating clear, structured, and effective prompts for AI systems.

3. **Business Prompt Engineering**
   Applies Prompt Engineering to practical business functions such as Marketing, HR, Finance, and Operations.

4. **25 Prompt Engineering Portfolio**
   Contains 25 practical prompts covering Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations, Zero-Shot, and Few-Shot Prompting.

## Objectives

* Understand practical applications of Artificial Intelligence.
* Develop effective and structured prompts.
* Apply AI to research and business scenarios.
* Improve the quality and relevance of AI-generated outputs.
* Understand different Prompt Engineering techniques.

## Key Skills

* AI Research
* Prompt Engineering
* Business Problem Solving
* Information Verification
* Fact-Checking
* Zero-Shot Prompting
* Few-Shot Prompting
* AI-Assisted Business Analysis

## Learning Outcome

These projects provide practical experience in using AI responsibly, designing effective prompts, applying AI to business problems, and evaluating AI-generated responses.

## Conclusion

Together, these projects demonstrate the practical application of **AI and Prompt Engineering** for research, business analysis, and real-world problem-solving.
